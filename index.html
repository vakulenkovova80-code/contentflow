ContentFlow Publisher

ContentFlow Publisher is a private desktop tool. Its owner uses it to publish his own short videos from his content studio to his own TikTok accounts, using the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

The app is not offered to the public and does not collect data of other TikTok users.
