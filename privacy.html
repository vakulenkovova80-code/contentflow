Privacy Policy — ContentFlow Publisher

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Data we receive from TikTok. When the owner connects his TikTok account, the app receives an access token and

basic profile information of that account (display name, avatar, follower count) and statistics of the owner's own

videos (views, likes).

2. How it is used. Only to publish the owner's own videos and to show the owner his own statistics.

3. Storage. Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or

used for advertising.

4. Other users. The app does not collect data of other TikTok users.

5. Deletion. The owner can disconnect the account at any time in the app or in TikTok settings; the stored tokens

are then deleted.

6. Contact. Telegram: https://t.me/only_poland
